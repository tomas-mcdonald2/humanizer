Margaret Ellison (born 1952) is a renowned American ceramicist whose work stands as a testament to the enduring interplay between traditional craft and modern design. Nestled in the heart of rural Vermont, her studio has become a vibrant hub for emerging artists.

Ellison studied at the Rhode Island School of Design, graduating in 1974. She is widely regarded as one of the most influential potters of her generation, and experts say her glazing techniques transformed the field. Her work has been featured in The New York Times, Architectural Digest, and Dwell.

While specific details about her early life are limited, she likely grew up in a family that valued artistic expression. In 2009 she received the Smithsonian Craft Award, highlighting her profound contributions to American ceramics.
