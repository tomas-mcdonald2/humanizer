I got laid off last Tuesday.

After 6 years at Northwind, my role was eliminated in a restructuring. I'm not saying it didn't sting — it did. But here's what I've learned in the 7 days since.

Your job is not your identity. Your network is your net worth. And every ending is a beginning in disguise.

I spent the week reaching out to 40 former colleagues. 31 replied. 12 offered intros. That's the power of showing up for people before you need them.

Read that again.

To everyone navigating a layoff right now: you are more than a title. Let's connect. 🙏
